What is raw honey from the Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve?

Honeymiood's raw honey is unpasteurised, unheated honey from a family apiary by the Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve in Gdynia. It comes from a single harvest season, never warmed above beehive temperature or pressure-filtered, so it keeps its natural pollen and the full structure of nectar gathered from the reserve's coastal meadows and forest.

A heritage that refuses to be rushed

It all began with great-great-grandmother Jadwiga, who kept the family's first hives. Today, Jarek tends the colonies on the quiet edge of Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve — where city life gives way to sea breeze and protected wild meadows.

“For us, each honey is a direct record of one specific summer, weather pattern, and bloom in the reserve. We add nothing and take nothing away.”
