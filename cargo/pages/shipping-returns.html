Returns

Under EU consumer law, you have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase without giving a reason. To return a product, email us at honeymiood@gmail.com within 14 days of receiving your order.

Because our products are food in sealed packaging, the right of withdrawal does not cover jars that have been opened after delivery, for health and hygiene reasons. Returns are possible for products in their original, unopened packaging.

Return shipping is paid by the buyer, unless the return is due to a product defect or an error on our part — in which case we cover the cost.

Quality complaints

If your order arrived damaged, or you have concerns about product quality, write to us with a description and, if possible, a photo — we review every case individually and respond as quickly as we can.
