What is raw honey from the Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve?

Honeymiood's raw honey is unpasteurised, unheated honey from a family apiary by the Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve in Gdynia. It comes from a single harvest season, never warmed above beehive temperature or pressure-filtered, so it keeps its natural pollen and the full structure of nectar gathered from the reserve's coastal meadows and forest.
