How do our seven honeys compare?

Every Honeymiood honey comes from a different bloom and harvest window at the same apiary by the Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve. They differ in colour, crystallisation speed, and flavour profile — from creamy, fast-crystallising rapeseed to acacia, which stays liquid for months.
