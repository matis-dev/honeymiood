About Us

Four generations, one apiary

We have been sharing beekeeping knowledge in our family for four generations. In every Honeymiood jar, you'll find 100% of the honey mood.

Generation I

Great-great-grandmother Jadwiga founded the first apiary and began the family craft.

Generation II

Great-grandfather Adam continued her work, passing the knowledge on.

Generation III

Grandfather Grzegorz still spends every free moment with his bee family.

Generation IV

Jarek — Beatka's husband, Wiesia and Grzegorz's son — now runs the apiary in the family garden in Gdynia.

Right by the reserve, with the sea beyond it

The apiary stands in our garden in Gdynia, right next to the Kępa Redłowska Nature Reserve, with the beach and sea just beyond it. The reserve is home to linden trees, acacias, evening primroses, cornflowers, clovers, herbs, dandelions and blackberries.

Our bees truly have a paradise here — reflected in their peaceful demeanour, they are incredibly calm. Our honey is truly aromatic and valuable thanks to this unique location.

Honeymiood was created to nurture the beekeeping craft and share the extraordinary taste of our honey.
